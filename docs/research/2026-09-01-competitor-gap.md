# Period tracker competitor gap review

Date: 2026-09-01

Only public official pages and public Google Play listings were reviewed. No competitor binary, private API, text, or artwork was copied.

## Current products

### Clue

- Public support lists period, PMS, ovulation, fertile-window, and cramps predictions.
- Analysis includes flow, cramps, basal-temperature charts, cycle overviews, and personal patterns.
- Calendar can filter up to three previously tracked experiences.
- Connect provides view-only cycle sharing.
- Sources: <https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/29048842612765-What-predictions-are-available-in-Clue>, <https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/30196149381789-How-can-I-filter-my-tracking-data-in-the-Calendar-View>, <https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/15007279508637-What-s-included-in-Clue-Plus>

### Samsung Health

- Period, fertile-window, and ovulation estimates use entered cycle history and optional wearable temperature data.
- New cycle data changes later predictions.
- Daily log covers symptoms, mood, and notes; settings expose cycle, period, and reproductive-state controls.
- Source: <https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10004563/>, <https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10004564/>

### Period Calendar

- Public listing covers period start/end, fertility, symptoms, mood, cervical signs, tests, weight, temperature, intimacy, notes, reminders, backup, anonymous use, and Wear OS.
- Source: <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.popularapp.periodcalendar>

### Flo

- Public pages cover cycle/symptom patterns, fertility, pregnancy, partner view, and an anonymous account mode.
- Selia's account-free local model already avoids account identifiers and remote health-data processing.
- Sources: <https://flo.health/>, <https://flo.health/product-tour/anonymous-mode>

### Stardust

- Public pages emphasize phase education, symptom patterns, wearable trends, partner mode, and pregnancy tracking.
- Source: <https://stardust.app/app-features>, <https://stardust.app/faq>

## Implemented now

- Coherent re-anchoring after new period data across period, fertility, ovulation, Today, Calendar, and calendar mirror.
- Calendar filters for locally recorded symptoms and experiences.
- Local symptom-by-phase patterns with sample thresholds and a non-diagnostic notice.
- Reduced calendar recomposition work.
- Device-transfer-only legacy backup rules.

## Intentionally not copied

- Competitor text, graphics, branding, layouts, rankings, subscription prompts, ads, or proprietary prediction algorithms.
- Cloud partner accounts and remote analytics.
- Astrology, guaranteed fertility claims, or cycle-synced diet claims.
- Pregnancy, medication-schedule, PCOS, PMDD, wearable, widget, and multi-profile products without separate requirements and validation.
