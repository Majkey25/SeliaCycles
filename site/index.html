Selia Cycles privacy policy

Updated October 5, 2026

Selia Cycles, package com.majkeylab.seliacycles, is published by Matěj Teplý, using the names Majkey and MajkeyLab. The publisher is the contact for this policy and for personal information sent to support: majkeylab@gmail.com.

Local data

Selia Cycles stores cycle records, optional daily details, optional age and body profile values, saved forecast baselines, reminders, profile choices, and display settings in private storage on the Android device. The app has no account, ads, analytics, telemetry, Selia cloud, or network permission. The app does not send cycle data to MajkeyLab.

Contraception records and reminders

Contraception records and reminder times stay in the selected profile. Android alarm and notification access is optional. Notifications use private visibility, but their appearance also depends on the device. Reminders do not confirm a dose or calculate contraceptive protection. Contraception records are not copied to external calendars.

Self-care suggestions

Self-care suggestions are selected locally from the selected profile's dates, estimated phase and optional symptom, pain, mood, energy, sleep and pregnancy entries. This does not send data to an AI service, MajkeyLab or a food provider. Using the app does not automatically consent to sharing health data. Read the self-care limits and sources.

Optional .pc import and export

Each local profile has a separate calendar database. Profile names and display modes stay on the device. Profiles are not password-protected accounts: anyone who can open the app can switch between them. Add another person's health data only with appropriate consent. Import and export apply only to the selected profile.

When you select a compatible .pc backup, Selia Cycles reads and previews it locally. Confirmed records are merged into private app storage and are not uploaded.

When you choose an export destination, Selia Cycles writes a .pc backup containing the selected profile's cycle records and app settings to Android's file provider. The file is not encrypted. If you choose a cloud-backed destination, that provider may upload the file under its own terms. Selia Cycles does not upload it to a MajkeyLab server. Anyone with access to the file may be able to read health data, so store and share it securely.

Optional calendar mirror

Sync is explicitly enabled per profile and never enabled by importing a backup. OFF is saved before cleanup, so failed deletion cannot restart synchronization. Copies include recorded history and at most two months ahead. Turning sync off removes copies identifiable as belonging to the selected profile. Ordinary calendar appointments are not interpreted as cycle records.

Delete Selia events offers a preview in a calendar you choose. Older copies may have lost their app identifiers during account synchronization. Matching titles are shown for your review, with nothing selected by default. A title alone cannot identify the original profile or person: select only copies you intend to delete. The preview can only include events available through the device calendar provider. For cloud-only copies, refresh the provider or use your calendar app. If deletion fails, sync stays off; restore calendar access and refresh the preview before retrying.

If you enable calendar mirroring, Selia Cycles requests Android calendar read/write permission and copies short labels for recorded periods, period estimates, fertile windows, and estimated ovulation into the calendar you select. Notes, symptoms, measurements, intimacy, and raw mood logs are never copied.

New copies include a Selia reference in their description: a local profile identifier, event type and date. This lets Selia recognize its copies even if the provider removes Android-specific app fields. The reference does not contain the profile name. Text added to the description in your calendar is retained when Selia updates the event.

The selected provider, such as Google Calendar, Outlook, Exchange, or a local calendar, controls account synchronization and access. Events are private by default. For view-only partner access, create and share a separate calendar in the provider, then select it in Selia. Selia cannot grant or enforce provider access. The provider's privacy terms then apply.

Device transfer

Android may copy the private database and settings directly during new-device setup. Selia Cycles supplies data only for a device-to-device transport. Normal cloud backup receives no app data. MajkeyLab cannot access the transfer.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Selia Cycles does not sell personal data. Enabling the optional calendar mirror is a user-directed disclosure to the chosen provider.

There is no Selia account to delete. Local records remain on your device until you edit or delete them using the controls below. The publisher cannot remotely read, recover or erase your device's records.

Settings > Data and transfer > Clear this calendar clears only the selected profile's records and settings. Manage profiles > Delete profile removes an additional profile and its records. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes all local profiles. Stop calendar sync removes identifiable copies for the selected profile while permission is available. Use Delete Selia events to review older copies with missing app identifiers. If permission was revoked or the app was uninstalled first, remove calendar copies in the calendar app.

Exported files and copies held by a selected file or calendar provider are not erased when you clear app data. Delete those copies separately through the provider.

External links and support

Privacy, source, and optional Buy Me a Coffee support links open in your browser. The destination website receives normal browser connection information and applies its own privacy policy. Selia Cycles does not attach health records to those links. If you email support, MajkeyLab receives the address and contents you choose to send. Do not include health records in support messages. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the contact address above.

Support correspondence is used to answer the request, resolve the reported problem and handle any related complaint. It is not used to enrol you in marketing. It is retained only while needed for those purposes, a related dispute or a legal duty. Sending support information is optional and is not required to use the app. Do not send health records or another person's personal data in a public issue.

Privacy rights

Where GDPR applies, the publisher relies on legitimate interests in answering support requests and resolving problems, Article 6(1)(f), and legal obligations when handling statutory requests, Article 6(1)(c). The offline app does not give the publisher access to your health records. Optional file and calendar transfers are controlled by your actions and the destination provider's terms; permission can be removed in Android settings. Removing permission does not erase copies already sent.

For personal information held by the publisher, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing without invalidating earlier lawful processing. Email majkeylab@gmail.com. Requests normally receive a response within one month, subject to applicable exceptions. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including the Czech Data Protection Authority. See its guide to privacy rights.

Providers you choose, including calendar, file, email and external support services, may process information outside your country. Their policies describe their processing and safeguards. The publisher cannot remotely erase provider-held copies. Local cycle estimates do not make legal or similarly significant decisions about you.

Medical disclaimer

Selia Cycles provides personal tracking, reconstructed forecasts, fertility estimates, phase education, and optional self-care timers. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It cannot confirm ovulation, predict symptoms with certainty, or provide contraception. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
