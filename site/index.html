Selia Cycles privacy policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Selia Cycles is published by MajkeyLab. Contact majkeylab@gmail.com about this policy.

Data stored by the app

Selia Cycles stores menstrual dates, flow, symptoms, mood, notes, reminder settings, and display settings in private storage on your Android device. The app has no account, ads, analytics, telemetry, or internet permission. MajkeyLab does not receive your cycle data.

Health Connect

Health Connect import is optional. If you tap Import data and grant permission, Selia Cycles reads menstrual period and flow history to add those records to your private calendar. The app does not read Health Connect in the background. It does not write, change, or delete Health Connect records.

Backups

Android Auto Backup may copy the app database to the Google or device backup account selected in Android settings. Android controls this service. MajkeyLab cannot access the backup.

Manual backup is optional. Selia Cycles encrypts the file with AES-GCM and a key derived from your password. You choose the destination in Android's system file picker. MajkeyLab cannot read the file or recover its password.

Sharing and sale

Selia Cycles does not share or sell personal data.

Retention and deletion

Use Settings > Delete all data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Manual backup files remain where you saved them until you delete them.

Medical disclaimer

Selia Cycles provides personal tracking and calendar estimates. It does not diagnose a condition, confirm ovulation, or provide contraception.
