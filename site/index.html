Selia Cycles privacy policy

Effective September 5, 2026

Selia Cycles is published by MajkeyLab. Contact majkeylab@gmail.com about this policy.

Local data

Selia Cycles stores cycle records, optional daily details, optional age and body profile values, saved forecast baselines, reminders, profile choices, and display settings in private storage on the Android device. The app has no account, ads, analytics, telemetry, Selia cloud, or network permission. MajkeyLab does not receive cycle data.

Optional .pc import and export

When you select a compatible .pc backup, Selia Cycles reads and previews it locally. Confirmed records are merged into private app storage and are not uploaded.

When you choose an export destination, Selia Cycles creates a local .pc backup containing cycle records and app settings. The export is not uploaded or encrypted. Anyone with access to the file may be able to read health data, so store and share it securely.

Optional calendar mirror

If you enable calendar mirroring, Selia Cycles requests Android calendar read/write permission and copies short labels for recorded periods, period estimates, fertile windows, and estimated ovulation into the calendar you select. Notes, symptoms, measurements, intimacy, and raw mood logs are never copied.

The selected provider, such as Google Calendar, Outlook, Exchange, or a local calendar, controls account synchronization and access. Events are private by default. For view-only partner access, create and share a separate calendar in the provider, then select it in Selia. Selia cannot grant or enforce provider access. The provider's privacy terms then apply.

Device transfer

Android may copy the private database and settings directly during new-device setup. Selia Cycles supplies data only for a device-to-device transport. Normal cloud backup receives no app data. MajkeyLab cannot access the transfer.

Sharing and deletion

Selia Cycles does not sell personal data. Enabling the optional calendar mirror is a user-directed disclosure to the chosen provider.

Use Settings > Delete all data, clear app storage, or uninstall to delete local records. Stop calendar sync removes Selia-created calendar events while permission is available. If permission was revoked or the app was uninstalled first, remove calendar copies in the calendar app.

Medical disclaimer

Selia Cycles provides personal tracking, reconstructed forecasts, fertility estimates, phase education, and optional self-care timers. It does not diagnose or treat a condition, confirm ovulation, predict symptoms with certainty, or provide contraception.
