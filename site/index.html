Selia Cycles privacy policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Selia Cycles is published by MajkeyLab. Contact majkeylab@gmail.com about this policy.

Local data

Selia Cycles stores cycle records, optional daily details, reminders, and display settings in private storage on the Android device. The app has no account, ads, analytics, telemetry, cloud sync, or network permission. MajkeyLab does not receive cycle data.

Device transfer

Android may copy the private database and settings directly during new-device setup. Selia Cycles supplies data only for a device-to-device transport. Normal cloud backup receives no app data. MajkeyLab cannot access the transfer.

Sharing and deletion

Selia Cycles does not sell or share personal data. Use Settings > Delete all data, clear app storage, or uninstall to delete local records.

Medical disclaimer

Selia Cycles provides personal tracking and calendar estimates. It does not diagnose a condition, confirm ovulation, or provide contraception.
