Selia Cycles privacy policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Selia Cycles is published by MajkeyLab. Contact majkeylab@gmail.com about this policy.

Data stored by the app

Selia Cycles stores menstrual dates, flow, symptoms, mood, notes, measurements, reminder settings, and display settings in private storage on your Android device. The app has no ads, analytics, or telemetry. Google sign-in and cloud sync are optional.

Optional Google account and cloud sync

If you sign in and enable sync, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account identifier, display name, and email address. Cloud Firestore stores your account identifier, display name, menstrual dates, and flow. Notes, mood, symptoms, weight, temperature, sleep, intimacy, and imported source details are excluded from the default cloud payload.

MajkeyLab administers the Firebase project and can technically access stored data. It is used only for sync, partner access, security, support, and deletion—not for ads, analytics, profiling, or sale.

A one-time invitation can grant a signed-in partner read-only access to menstrual dates and flow. The owner can revoke access at any time.

Health Connect

Health Connect import is optional. Selia Cycles reads menstrual period and flow history into the calendar only after permission. It does not read in the background or write Health Connect records. Imported dates and flow are synced only if you separately enable Google cloud sync.

Backups

Android Auto Backup may copy the app database to the Google or device backup account selected in Android settings. Android controls this service. MajkeyLab cannot access the backup.

Manual backup is optional. Selia Cycles encrypts the file with AES-GCM and a key derived from your password. You choose the destination in Android's system file picker. MajkeyLab cannot read the file or recover its password.

Sharing and sale

Selia Cycles does not sell personal data. Google/Firebase processes the optional account and synced fields described above. A partner receives only the read-only fields explicitly shared by the owner.

Retention and deletion

Use Settings > Delete all data for local records. Use Google account and partner > Delete cloud copy for synced data, invitations, and partner access. Manual backup files remain where you saved them until you delete them.

Medical disclaimer

Selia Cycles provides personal tracking and calendar estimates. It does not diagnose a condition, confirm ovulation, or provide contraception.
