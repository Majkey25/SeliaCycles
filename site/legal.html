Terms of use

Selia Cycles is a free Android calendar published under the MajkeyLab name. It has no paid features, subscriptions, advertising, or app account. You do not exchange health records for access to the app.

Record and edit your own cycle information, or another person's information only with appropriate permission. Local profiles are not locked accounts. Anyone who can open the app can switch profiles. Optional export and calendar mirroring disclose data to the destination you choose; review its access and sharing settings.

The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Calendar estimates cannot confirm ovulation, guarantee a pregnancy outcome, or provide contraception. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

These terms describe the rules for using the app. Using the app does not grant consent to upload, share or sell health records. Optional disclosures still require your own action and applicable Android permissions. These terms do not replace a legally required consent or waive mandatory rights.

You control your records. Import/export and deletion apply to the selected profile. Keep backups securely; exported files are not encrypted. Clearing the app does not erase external files or copies kept by a calendar provider.

Self-care, food and activity suggestions

Self-care is general educational information, not an individual diagnosis, treatment plan, prescription or medical assessment. No suggestion, food, nutrient, exercise, timer or cycle estimate is guaranteed to reduce pain, improve mood, relieve symptoms or produce any other result. Benefits differ between people and between cycles. A displayed suggestion does not establish the cause of a symptom or rule out illness.

Suggestions are selected locally from the chosen day, estimated phase, bleeding-day context and optional symptoms, pain, mood, energy, sleep and pregnancy information that you record. Strong pain and certain pregnancy or menopause entries take priority over ordinary activity suggestions. Missing information is not proof that an activity is safe for you. The app cannot identify an emergency from incomplete records.

Pregnancy context uses your profile and the most recent valid pregnancy-test entry on or before the selected day. An earlier positive result remains relevant until a later valid result is recorded; a profile set to pregnant still takes priority. These entries do not establish a diagnosis. Review outdated information and consult a professional if test results conflict.

Daily meal rotation provides variety. It is not evidence that a particular food is needed on a particular cycle day. Bananas, oats, nuts and seeds contain magnesium, but this does not establish that eating a banana on day two treats menstrual pain. The app does not recommend supplement doses, restrictive diets or changing prescribed medication. Use foods you tolerate, consider allergies, and follow any personal dietary, fluid or exercise restrictions from your healthcare professional.

Activity durations are optional timers, not clinical doses. Keep warmth comfortable and wrapped, avoid sleeping with a heat source, move within a comfortable range, and stop if symptoms worsen. Seek prompt professional advice for severe, new or persistent symptoms, bleeding in pregnancy or after menopause, or symptoms disrupting daily life. Sudden extreme pain, fainting, neurological symptoms or heavy bleeding may require urgent help. Do not delay care because a suggestion appears in this app.

The app retains relevant safety instructions beside the activity or recorded concern. General limitations are collected here to avoid repeating them on every screen. Nothing here excludes liability or statutory consumer rights where the law does not permit that exclusion. For questions about an individual medical condition, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
